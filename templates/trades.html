How this works

Pick two teams and tap the players each one sends. A player's value is how many more points a week he's projected to score than a replacement-level starter at his position, over the rest of the season: bye weeks still ahead and injuries count against it. The verdict compares the value each team gets. The lineup check plays out each remaining week with and without the deal, including who a team would have to drop or could pick up, to show what it does to each team's starting lineup.
